Before you start — key conditions

General information

  • The abstract submission deadline is Friday 27th November 2026.
  • Abstracts will be scored against the 5 subheadings under the main body section of the submission and selected by peer review for inclusion in the conference as a digital poster, moderated poster discussion or 10-minute oral presentation.
  • The author submitting the abstract must ensure that they, or one of the other named co-authors, will be available to attend the conference in person. By submitting this abstract you agree to present it in the format assigned to you (digital poster, moderated poster discussion or oral presentation).
  • You are invited to submit multiple abstracts on different topics should you wish to.
  • Abstracts must be relevant to the field of respiratory physiotherapy; they may be research-focused, education, leadership or clinically-focused (including service evaluation/clinical audit). Projects should be completed and should not be in the early stages of development.
  • Student physiotherapists' abstract submissions showcasing their work are encouraged.

Abstract conditions

  • The main body of the abstract (background, aim/objectives, methods, results and conclusions/implications for practice) must be no more than 400 words in total (including subheadings).
  • Approval: please include ethical/governance or R&D/audit approval as required. Most projects (including audits/service evaluations) will require approval of some kind. Please do not leave this section blank. If you think your project did not require any approvals, explain why this is the case in the section. If unsure, please contact research@acprc.org.uk.
  • Please do not include tables, figures or appendices.
  • Please include a maximum of three references using Vancouver format (see website guidance for details). References are not included in the wordcount.

Please note

  • All expenses associated with submission and presentation of the abstract, including conference registration fees, are the responsibility of the presenting author.
  • All abstracts selected for presentation will be considered for publication in the Winter 2028 Supplement of the ACPRC Journal — submission of an abstract constitutes permission to publish an abstract. Any updates to data can be presented at the conference, however written abstract submissions will remain unchanged for later publication purposes. Suitability for publication will be based upon ethical standards and quality of work.
  • If the outcome of your submission requires changes, additional information or is rejected following peer review, a new submission will be required via the submission platform. Comments/feedback will be provided to support your resubmission. You will be able to download your original submission upon completion.
