Annual Conference 2027

Award Nomination

Nominate someone for an ACPRC award

We know that our members are committed to delivering high quality care to patients, teaching the next generation of physiotherapists, contributing to the evidence base and leading our profession — much of this work goes unnoticed and deserves recognition. Nominate yourself or a colleague who has made a particularly impactful contribution to our speciality over the last two years.

Nominations close 23:59, Sunday 28 February 2027
Before you start — key conditions

General information

  • There is no limit to how many people you can nominate.
  • All five award categories are open to any ACPRC member, apart from Newcomer of the Year, which is open only to those who have qualified in the last 3 years.
  • Nominations will be considered by an awards subgroup, consisting of the ACPRC chair, vice chair, and three further committee members.

Timeline

  • Nominations open Monday 1st February 2027, closing Sunday 28th February 2027.
  • The awards subgroup meets to review nominations on Thursday 11th March 2027.
  • Shortlisted nominees are notified from Thursday 1st April 2027, with the winner announced at the conference.
